# Story Construction: short visual stories

Use this skill when you build or review an emojibench story.

## Format

A story is 3 to 5 panels. Each panel has one asset and one caption.
The story has a title and an audience level (kids, general, researcher).

## Rules

1. Only approved assets go into stories. Drafts and rejected assets
   are not story material.
2. Each panel says one thing. Short sentences beat long ones.
3. Keep narrative continuity: each panel follows from the one before.
   The story needs a beginning, a middle, and an end.
4. Captions match the audience level (see the scientific-review skill
   for audience guidance).
5. Every caption's factual claims follow the scientific-review rules:
   observations have sources, interpretations are labeled, analogy
   stays playful.
6. Export includes story JSON plus the panel images, so the story is
   shareable and reviewable without the app.
7. Do not assert that a whimsical illustration is scientific evidence.
   Where a panel references real evidence, the panel record points to
   the evidence source (asset id, dataset, citation).

## Example structure

Title: "A Macrophage Saves the Day" (audience: kids)

1. Meet the macrophage. (caption: friendly introduction)
2. Trouble arrives: a germ shows up. (caption: playful setup)
3. The macrophage engulfs the germ. (caption: what engulfing means,
   as analogy)
4. The tissue is safe again. (caption: resolution)

## Review checklist

- 3-5 panels, one idea each, beginning/middle/end present?
- All assets approved?
- Captions match audience level?
- Claims sourced or clearly playful?
- Export JSON validates against the story schema?
